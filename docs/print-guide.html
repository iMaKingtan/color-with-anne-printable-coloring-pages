A two-minute setup

How to print a coloring page cleanly

Browser and printer names differ, but these settings prevent most clipped edges, tiny drawings, and accidental color ink use.

  1. 1

    Choose the correct paper

    Select US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) or A4 (210 x 297 mm) to match the paper loaded in your printer.

  2. 2

    Use portrait orientation

    Color with Anne sheets are laid out vertically. Portrait gives the drawing the largest usable area.

  3. 3

    Fit to the printable area

    Choose "Fit," "Fit to page," or a scale near 95%. Avoid "Fill," which can crop the outer lines.

  4. 4

    Switch off extra headers

    Disable browser headers and footers if the preview adds a URL, date, or page number.

  5. 5

    Use grayscale or black ink

    The artwork contains only black outlines. Grayscale mode avoids using color cartridges unnecessarily.

If the edge is still clipped

Reduce scale to 90-95% or select your printer's "printable area" option. Most home printers cannot place ink all the way to the paper edge.
